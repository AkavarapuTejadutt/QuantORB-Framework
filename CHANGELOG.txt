CHANGELOG
=========

1.0.0  (2026-09-25)
  First packaged release.
  - Opening range 09:15-09:45, breakout entries until the 12:10 close,
    stop = previous candle low/high honoured only on candle close,
    target = RISK_REWARD x stop distance checked on live ticks,
    square-off at 15:10, MAX_TRADES_PER_DAY limit.
  - FUTURES mode (Nifty futures with automatic monthly rollover) and
    STOCK mode (capital x leverage sizing).
  - PAPER_TRADING switch, 24/7 runner with restart-safe daily state,
    replay tool for past candles, automatic TOTP login.
  - Fix: last eligible candle (closing at 12:10) is now always processed
    before the day is declared finished.
  - Fix: WebSocket close no longer triggers a reconnect after a
    deliberate stop.
